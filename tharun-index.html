About Me

Curiosity is where every build begins.

I’m Tharun Manjunath, a Computer Science and Engineering student at R.V. College of Engineering, Bengaluru. I’m fascinated by the space between an ambitious idea and a working solution.

My journey so far has taken me through AI-powered mental-health conversations, renewable-energy-driven smart irrigation, and legal technology that makes complex concepts easier to understand. Each project challenges me to think beyond code — about people, systems, and the problems worth solving.

Outside development, sports have shaped my discipline, resilience, and competitive mindset. Inside development, I’m constantly learning, experimenting, breaking things, and building them better.

I’m not here to claim I’ve figured everything out. I’m here to keep getting better — one meaningful project at a time.
